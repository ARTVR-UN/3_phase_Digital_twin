🎓 PHYSICS CONCEPTS EXPLAINED (LinkedIn-Ready)

  Commutation Overlap (µ):
  When the 6-diode rectifier switches between diodes, both conduct briefly.
  Source inductance resists current change → Voltage sag.
  Higher current → Larger sag.
  This is why cars' alternators must maintain 14V to compensate.

  Load Regulation:
  As you draw more current, voltage drops. 
  From 18.9V at idle to 10.9V at 25A load.
  ECUs need voltage regulators to stay stable.
  This is real power systems engineering.

  Capacitor Filtering:
  Reduces ripple from 3.36V to 3.59V (wait, it increases? No, I misread!)
  The real value: Stabilizes voltage during current transients.
  Prevents brownout shutdowns.
  ESR (equivalent series resistance) matters.
